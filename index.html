// 01. THE MANIFESTO

Why We Reject Alphanumeric Serials

Human brains evolved to remember myths, stories, and poetic names—not spreadsheet barcodes. Calling a massive, burning sun "PSR B1919+21" is like naming your dog "Canine-0482."

Even worse, centralized bodies claim an "address number" helps point telescopes, yet you still have to query a database just to know if it's East or West. Meanwhile, predatory websites sell fake certificates for $50 that nobody recognizes.

The Civilian Astronomy Union (CAU) bridges the gap: an open, democratic, zero-cost registry run by independent sky-watchers worldwide.

// 03. THE CHARTER

Three Golden Rules of Naming

To keep our registry respected, poetic, and free from chaos, every entry follows three simple principles.

RULE 01 // RESPECT

Honor Ancient Names

We never overwrite classic, culturally beloved names like Sirius, Vega, or Betelgeuse. Our mission targets unnamed stars trapped behind alphanumeric catalog codes.

RULE 02 // DIGNITY

Poetic & Meaningful

No corporate advertisements, spam, or offensive slurs. Every proposed name should carry a story, visual inspiration, or timeless human emotion worthy of a sun.

RULE 03 // DEMOCRACY

Consensus by Vote

When multiple observers propose names for the same star, the community decides via public X (Twitter) polls. The people's choice becomes permanent history.
